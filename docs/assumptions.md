# Assumptions and limits

This is a video-derived spatial study, not a measured survey, architectural drawing or verified marketing plan. The model, SVG plan, dossiers and evidence register are generated from the same provisional property record.

## Source and method

- Source: the supplied hand-held walkthrough, 512 × 910, 30 fps, 23.2 seconds, with AAC audio.
- The video is embedded unchanged in the site. Reference stills are extracts used for comparison.
- Geometry was reconstructed by eye from the visible sequence. No camera tracking, photogrammetry, depth capture, laser measurement or metric calibration was used.
- Relationships between rooms, openings and large furniture items are based on what is visible in the footage or stated in the owner brief.
- The plan is a diagram, not a construction document. It is drawn from the same room polygons and wall runs as the 3D model.

## Scale and footprint

The model uses an assumed envelope of 8.0 × 5.2 m with 2.7 m walls. The envelope was chosen to produce a plausible compact one-bedroom arrangement; it is not a measured footprint and should not be read as the flat's actual size.

The 400 sq ft figure is owner-reported and unverified. It does not drive the modelled geometry.

Wall thickness, ceiling height, window and door widths, balcony depth and the exact position of the flat within its building are unknown. The north arrow is diagrammatic; the footage does not establish a reliable real-world orientation.

## Room-by-room assumptions

### Living and entrance hall

The entrance opens into a compact hall that doubles as the living area. The glossy floor, television, dark storage, clock, router, blue stool and wide cased opening are visible. The hall/far-zone boundary is observable, but the measured room size and exact wall positions are not.

### Far zone

The footage shows a counter run, jars and utensils, a tall dark appliance, black shelving, a water dispenser and a blue curtain. These observations do not establish the zone's function. It is deliberately labelled **Function unknown** and is not asserted to be a kitchen, utility room or other specific use.

### Wash alcove

A pedestal washbasin and mirror are glimpsed in an open alcove, with a closed white door beyond. Only the open alcove is visible. The arrangement is inferred from the footage; plumbing positions and the space behind the closed door are unknown.

### Closed room

A closed white door suggests a further room, but its interior was never filmed. Its depth, function and contents are unknown. The model leaves it closed and unlabelled beyond the door.

### Bedroom

The bedroom is confirmed as separate from the living area. Visible elements include a blue upholstered daybed/sofa on a dark wood base, grey patterned curtains, a striped rug, an acoustic guitar, low dark storage, a green floor cushion and a white balcony door. Furniture positions are visually estimated.

The daybed is modelled along the bedroom's balcony side so the balcony-door zone remains clear, consistent with the visible sequence.

### Utility balcony

The balcony opening, fine white safety mesh, dark frames, handrail, low parapet and laundry basin with mop are visible. The opening is confirmed; its width, depth, clear height and facade relationship are unmeasured.

## Model interpretation

- Surfaces are material interpretations, not sampled real finishes.
- Furniture not clearly visible is avoided. Generic forms are used only where the footage supports a shape or category.
- Lighting, reflections and colours are presentational and not photometric.
- The dresser, drying rack, appliances and other small elements are approximate placements.
- Rooflines, exterior elevations, neighbouring buildings and the world outside the flat are not reconstructed.

## Confidence vocabulary

- **Confirmed**: directly visible in the walkthrough or stated in the owner brief.
- **Inferred**: a reasonable reading supported by the footage, but not directly proven.
- **Unknown**: not visible or not established; no function or dimension is supplied.
- **Provisional**: all geometry, positions and dimensions remain estimates until a measured survey is available.

## What would improve the model

A measured floor plan, ceiling-height readings, door and window dimensions, a compass bearing, additional photographs of the far zone and the interior behind the closed white door, and the actual balcony dimensions would materially improve the reconstruction. Until then, the unknowns remain visible by design.

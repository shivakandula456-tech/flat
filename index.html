Method & limits

Built from a walkthrough, not a survey.

No floor plan, dimensions or camera tracking were supplied. The model uses a visual reconstruction of a 23.2-second, 512 × 910 hand-held video. Its 8.0 × 5.2 m envelope is a planning assumption.

  • No photogrammetry or LiDAR
  • No verified wall, opening or ceiling dimensions
  • No compass orientation established
  • Far-zone function intentionally unresolved

The reported 400 sq ft figure is owner-supplied and was not used to derive the model.
